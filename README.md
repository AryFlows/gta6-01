Vellora Bay is a procedural open-world crime game that runs entirely in the browser. Drive through a living city full of traffic and pedestrians, pick from five car types, and build your wanted level from 0 to 5 as police chases heat up. Take on story jobs and fight a rival gang for turf across the city, with pistol combat, a full HUD and minimap, localStorage saves that keep your progress, and WebAudio sound effects throughout.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 — serve.ts serves the game/ directory.
