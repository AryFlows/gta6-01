Vellora Bay is a procedural open-world crime game that runs entirely in the browser. Cruise a drivable city alive with traffic and pedestrians in one of five car types, then stir up trouble and feel the heat: a wanted level from 0 to 5 escalates police chases as your crimes pile up. Work through story jobs and a rival-gang turf chain, trade shots in pistol combat, and keep an eye on the HUD and minimap while you carve out your corner of the bay. Progress is saved to localStorage, and WebAudio sound effects bring the streets to life.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 — the server serves the game from `game/`.
