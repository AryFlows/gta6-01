Vellora Bay is a procedural open-world crime game that runs entirely in the browser. Cruise a living city full of traffic and pedestrians, steal and drive five different car types, and climb the wanted ladder from 0 to 5 as police chases escalate around you. Take on story jobs, fight a rival gang for turf across the map, and shoot it out with a pistol when things get loud. A HUD and minimap keep you oriented, progress saves to localStorage, and WebAudio sound effects bring the streets to life.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 — the server serves the game/ directory.
